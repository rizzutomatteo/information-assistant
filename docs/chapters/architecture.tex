\section{Architecture Overview}
\label{sec:architecture-overview}\index{architecture}\index{login}\index{guest}\index{Keycloak}\index{Caddy}

A question crosses eight layers (Figure~\ref{fig:architecture}): from the web chat to access
control, the input guardrail, the LangGraph\index{LangGraph} orchestrator~\cite{langgraph}, the
sources, the generation and the output guardrail, up to the answer or an operator. Login is optional: a guest gets general answers from the
public documents, a customer also gets answers on their own policy. Everything is free: one
server, plus Groq's free \acs{api} for the two large models (Figure~\ref{fig:overview-strip}).
